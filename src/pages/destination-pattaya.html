The Coast, Built for Groups.

With its beachfront resorts, island excursions and wide choice of event venues, Pattaya offers the space and variety to bring people together. It is a compelling choice for corporate incentives, large group programmes, family celebrations and coastal getaways within reach of Bangkok.

The Enchantive Advantage

When you're bringing a large group together, the details matter. We coordinate accommodation, transport, event production and supplier schedules to help the entire programme run smoothly, whether the occasion calls for a conference, an incentive trip or a celebration by the sea.

Make the Coast Your Own

Meetings, Incentives & Events

From resort-based conferences and awards dinners to beachside celebrations and themed evenings, we develop programmes around your goals and the scale of your group.

Team Experiences

Bring colleagues together through boat excursions, island days and thoughtfully designed group activities that encourage connection and shared achievement.

Leisure & Island Escapes

Balance the energy of the city with coastal experiences, island hopping, gardens and cultural attractions selected to suit your guests.

Private Celebrations & VIP Stays

From seaside parties and family gatherings to private boat charters and villa stays, we help create more personal ways to enjoy the coast.

Every Detail, Coordinated

We plan road transfers, vehicle requirements, arrival windows and group movements in advance. For boat trips and outdoor events, we also consider weather conditions and agree on suitable alternatives where needed.

Share your dates, guest numbers and vision. We'll help you make the most of the coast.
