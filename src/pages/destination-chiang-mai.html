Chiang Mai

02 / 05

Mountains, Craft & Slow Mornings.

In northern Thailand, life takes on a different rhythm. Chiang Mai invites guests to look beyond the usual itinerary, with mountain scenery, Lanna heritage, local craftsmanship and a growing appreciation for wellness and meaningful travel.

The Enchantive Advantage

The character of Chiang Mai lies in its people as much as its landscapes. We bring guests closer to local culture through carefully selected guides, artisans, hosts and experiences that offer a more personal connection to the destination.

Discover the North

Culture & Private Exploration

Explore the old city's temples, discover local crafts, join hands-on workshops or venture into the surrounding countryside with knowledgeable local guides.

Incentives & Corporate Gatherings

Bring teams together through cultural experiences, thoughtfully designed group activities, distinctive dining settings and celebrations framed by the beauty of northern Thailand.

Wellness & Nature

Create space to slow down with wellness-led stays, mountain escapes and carefully selected wildlife experiences that prioritise responsible practices.

Weddings & Celebrations

From garden settings to mountain-view occasions, Chiang Mai offers a beautiful backdrop for celebrations shaped around your vision and, where appropriate, local traditions.

A Different Pace, Thoughtfully Planned

We coordinate flights or onward transfers, airport pickups and the timing of each day's activities. For journeys into the hills and countryside, we allow for realistic travel times so guests can enjoy the experience without feeling rushed.

Tell us the pace your guests prefer. We'll find the northern Thailand that suits them.
