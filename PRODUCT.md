## Stack

## Users
Primary: B2B travel trade and corporate buyers (global partners, agents, planners) sourcing Thailand ground handling, MICE and events. Secondary: direct luxury travellers and couples planning leisure trips, weddings and celebrations in Thailand. (Confirmed by user.)

## Product Purpose
Enchantive Asia is a Destination Management Partner for Thailand. It delivers tailor-made travel solutions and ground-handling services for global partners. Founded by professionals from Asia's luxury travel sector. Success: enquiries via the contact page from trade and private clients.

## Positioning
Luxury-sector pedigree: service standards from Asia's luxury travel industry applied to Thailand DMC work (inferred from About page copy; confirm with client).

## Operating Context
Site sections: Services, MICE, Leisure, Luxe Experiences, Celebrations (11 sub-pages), Destinations (Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Ayutthaya, Hua Hin, Pattaya, Weddings), Gallery, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Edit SCSS and `build-pages.mjs`, not generated HTML by hand.
- Fonts must be self-hosted (CSP headers in place; no third-party font CDN).
- Working tree has uncommitted changes from earlier work; preloader recently removed.

## Brand Commitments
Keep logo, brand blue `#0658a7`, and existing copy unchanged. Client wants a single font family site-wide that feels luxurious and not like a generic default or "AI" font (user-stated, binding).

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles
1. Luxury through restraint: refinement over decoration.
2. Serve both trade (clarity, credibility) and private clients (desire).
3. Content and brand assets are fixed; presentation improves.
4. One typographic voice across every page.
