1. FIT — Fully Independent Travel

Your journey. Your pace. Your preferences.

For travellers who prefer a more personal way to explore, our FIT experiences are designed around individual interests, preferred schedules and travel styles. 2. Fixed Departures

Great destinations. Shared adventures.

Discover Thailand through planned itineraries that bring people together. Our fixed-departure journeys offer a structured way to explore destinations, enjoy local experiences and make new connections, with the convenience of having key arrangements organised in advance.

Perfect for: Solo travellers, friends, small groups and travellers who enjoy organised trips.

Bring people together. Make it memorable.

Some of the best experiences happen when people come together. From group getaways and themed gatherings to social outings and private group activities, we help organise occasions that encourage connection, celebration and shared memories.

Perfect for: Friends, communities, interest groups and private gatherings.

4. Family Holidays, Thoughtfully Curated.

Family Holidays, Thoughtfully Curated. The best family holidays are the ones where everyone has something to look forward to. From exceptional stays and private excursions to experiences that bring generations together, Enchantive Asia creates family journeys around the interests, preferences and pace of each family.

5. Private Getaways

Escape the Ordinary.

Private retreats and exceptional escapes, designed around you.

Some escapes call for complete privacy, a change of pace and the freedom to experience a destination on your own terms.

Enchantive Asia curates private getaways for those who value individuality, comfort and experiences beyond the usual. From secluded villas and exceptional resorts to private yacht experiences and carefully selected destinations, every journey is shaped around how you want to spend your time.

Private Travel. Personally Taken Care Of.

A more personal approach to travel, with every detail handled around you.

For travellers who expect more than standard arrangements, Enchantive Asia offers a personalised approach to travel across Thailand.
